What is an eSIM?

A digital SIM that is installed on your phone with a QR code. No plastic card needed.

Can I make calls or send SMS?

No. These eSIMs give mobile data only.

Will it work on my phone?

Your phone must support eSIM and be carrier unlocked. Check this before buying.

How do I get my eSIM?

After you pay from your wallet, WhatsApp opens so you can receive your QR code. You can also find your order again under "My eSIMs".

How do I install it?

On your phone open Settings, go to Mobile data / Cellular, choose Add eSIM, then scan the QR code you received.
