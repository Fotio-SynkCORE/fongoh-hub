How to add your eSIM

Three quick steps. No SIM tray needed.

1

Open your phone settings

iPhone: Settings → Mobile Service. Android: Settings → Network & internet → SIMs.

2

Tap Add eSIM and scan your QR code

Point your camera at the QR code you received, or choose to enter the details manually.

3

Turn it on and enable data roaming

Turn on the new line and switch on data roaming for it. When you arrive, your phone connects.

Check compatibility
